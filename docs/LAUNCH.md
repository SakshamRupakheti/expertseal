# ExpertSeal launch caption

The algorithm picks who gets your attention. You decide who earns your trust. 🛡️

I just open-sourced **ExpertSeal** — a browser extension that helps you research the person behind the advice.

Click **Check credentials** to explore career experience, education, publications and social presence, with links to the sources. Public LinkedIn results can help you investigate a career claim; they aren't verified records.

It's running in Edge on real TikTok and YouTube profiles. Bring your own API key, use [Tavily's free tier](https://www.tavily.com/pricing) within its limits, and reopen cached reports without another search. MIT licensed. No ExpertSeal subscription.

This is an early build. Missing evidence stays missing. Unrelated people stay separate. You make the judgment.

Try it. Break it. Open an issue. Help build something worth trusting.
